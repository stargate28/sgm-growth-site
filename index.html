Questions

Things owners usually ask us

Is the website really included in $299 a month?

Yes. We design and build your website, then host it, take care of it, and keep it updated as part of your plan.

Is there a contract?

No. The SGM Growth Plan is month-to-month, and you can cancel anytime. If you leave, we hand over your website, domain and customer data.

Do I pay for ads separately?

Your ad budget goes straight to Google or Meta, so you always decide how much to spend. We handle the setup and management.

Will the AI sound like a robot?

Not at all. Your assistants are trained on your business and speak naturally. Anything they can't handle is passed to you or saved as a message.

Can calls still come to my phone?

Of course. The receptionist can screen calls and send the important ones straight to your cell.

I already have a website. Can you work with it?

Yes. We can improve what you have or build you something new, whichever helps you grow more.

What kinds of businesses do you help?

We work with local businesses across Central Florida, including HVAC and home services, real estate, jewelers, salons and lash studios, restaurants and bars, tax preparers, med spas, auto shops, and many more.
