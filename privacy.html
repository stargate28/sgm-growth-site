SGM Growth Solutions is a DBA of Stargate Management LLC, a Florida company ("SGM", "we", "us"). This policy explains what information we collect through our website (sgmgrowth.com), our chat assistant, email, text messages, phone calls and our social media pages, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, business name, phone number, email address, quiz answers, appointment details and anything you write in our chat, forms, emails or messages.

  • Social media: when you interact with our Facebook or Instagram pages, we see what those platforms show page owners (for example, comments and messages you send us).

How we use it

  • To answer your questions, book and confirm strategy calls, and send reminders.
  • To follow up about the services you asked about.

  • To operate, secure and improve our website and services.

AI assistants

Our website chat and email are answered by AI assistants, and a person on our team can step in at any time. Messages are processed by our AI service provider only to generate replies. We review conversations to make sure our assistants give accurate, helpful answers.

Text messages

If you agree to receive texts from us, we will text you about your request or appointment. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. We do not sell, rent or share your mobile number or text messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business (for example, website hosting, email, calendar, texting, AI and social media platforms), only as needed to provide those services, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep information for as long as we need it to serve you and run our business, then delete it. You can ask us to delete it sooner (see below).

Your choices and data deletion

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we have about you, including any information connected to our Facebook or Instagram pages. Email Stargatemgmtllc@gmail.com or call (407) 495-2217 with the subject "Delete my data", and we will confirm once it is done, usually within 30 days.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes
